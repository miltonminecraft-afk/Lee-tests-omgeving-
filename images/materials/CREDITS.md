# Material sources

Photographic textures from Poly Haven, licensed CC0:

- [Plaster Grey 04](https://polyhaven.com/a/plaster_grey_04), Rob Tuytel.
- [Marble 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01), Rob Tuytel.
- [Wood Table 001](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table_001), Dimitrios Savva and Rico Cilliers.

Original 1K diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness JPEGs are bundled here. Higher-resolution URLs were obtained from Poly Haven's files API and recorded in `content/materials.json`. The 8K setting uses 8192px color maps and 2K detail maps. Painted plaster uses a white mineral-paint tint in the shader; source images are unchanged.

Three.js postprocessing, RoomEnvironment and RoundedBoxGeometry are from the official three 0.180.0 npm package, under the MIT license in `vendor/LICENSE`.
